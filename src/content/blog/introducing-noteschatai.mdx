# Introducing NotesChatAI

We're excited to announce **NotesChatAI** — an AI study assistant that remembers everything you upload, forever.

## The Problem

As students and researchers, we've all been there:

1. **Upload a textbook to ChatGPT** → Have a great conversation → Session ends → **All context lost**
2. **Create a NotebookLM notebook** → Add 50 sources max → Need a 51st → **Create another notebook** → Can't search across them
3. **Build a knowledge base in Obsidian/Notion** → Manual linking → **Hours of maintenance** → Still no AI chat

## Our Solution

NotesChatAI takes a fundamentally different approach:

### 🧠 **Persistent, Unified Memory**
Every document you upload becomes part of your permanent knowledge base. No 50-source limits. No notebook isolation. Chat across your entire library.

### 🔍 **Global Semantic Search**
Vector-powered search finds concepts across all your documents — not just keyword matches. Filter by source, date, type, or topic.

### 📚 **Citations You Can Trust**
Every answer cites exact passages. Hover to preview. Click to jump to the source. Works across multiple documents simultaneously.

### 🎧 **Audio Overviews That Work**
Generate podcast-style summaries with **full transcripts, chapters, speed control, and a saved library**. No regenerating from scratch.

### 🗺️ **Interactive Mind Maps**
Visualize how your knowledge connects. Click nodes to explore. Query subgraphs. Export as Mermaid or images.

### 🃏 **Spaced Repetition Built In**
Auto-generate flashcards (cloze, basic, reversed). FSRS algorithm schedules reviews. Export to Anki.

## Technical Foundation

- **Cloudflare-native**: Workers, Pages, D1, Vectorize, R2, Queues
- **Models**: Gemini 1.5 Flash/Pro (chat), bge-base-en-v1.5 (embeddings)
- **Open by default**: REST + GraphQL API, webhooks, TypeScript/Python/Go SDKs
- **Privacy-first**: Your data never trains models. Self-hosted option available.

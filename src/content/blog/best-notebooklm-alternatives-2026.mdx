## 2. ChatGPT with Files

**What it is:** OpenAI's ChatGPT with file upload and memory features.

**Pros:**
- Excellent reasoning with GPT-4o and o1 models
- File upload (PDF, images, code)
- Memory feature remembers past conversations
- Web search for current information

**Cons:**
- No persistent knowledge base — files are session-scoped
- No cross-document search or citations
- No study tools (flashcards, quizzes)
- $20/mo for Plus, $200/mo for Pro
- No API for study workflows

**Best for:** Quick Q&A on individual documents, not for building a knowledge base.

## 3. Obsidian + AI Plugins

**What it is:** Local-first note-taking app with community AI plugins (Copilot, Smart Connections, etc.).

**Pros:**
- 100% local — complete data ownership
- Powerful linking and graph view
- Huge plugin ecosystem
- Free for personal use

**Cons:**
- No built-in AI — requires third-party plugins
- No audio overviews
- No auto-generated flashcards (need Anki integration)
- Manual setup and maintenance
- No cloud sync without paid add-ons

**Best for:** Privacy-focused users comfortable with DIY setups.

## 4. Notion AI

**What it is:** Notion's built-in AI assistant for writing, summarizing, and Q&A.

**Pros:**
- Excellent document organization
- AI writing assistant built in
- Databases, wikis, and project management
- Good team collaboration

**Cons:**
- AI is an add-on ($10/mo extra on top of Notion plans)
- No RAG (AI doesn't deeply understand your documents)
- No audio, flashcards, or quiz features
- Limited to Notion's ecosystem

**Best for:** Teams already using Notion for project management.

## 5. Perplexity AI

**What it is:** AI-powered search engine with file upload and collections.

**Pros:**
- Excellent web search with citations
- File upload and collection organization
- Pro Search for deeper reasoning
- Clean, fast interface

**Cons:**
- No persistent knowledge base across sessions
- Collections are limited (50 files max on free)
- No study tools
- No audio overviews
- $20/mo for Pro

**Best for:** Research and web search, not for studying your own documents.

## 6. Elicit

**What it is:** AI research assistant focused on academic papers.

**Pros:**
- Excellent for literature reviews
- Automatic paper summaries and key findings
- Extraction tables across papers
- Semantic search for academic content

**Cons:**
- Academic papers only (no textbooks, notes, or audio)
- No flashcards, quizzes, or study modes
- Limited to research workflows
- $12/mo for Plus

**Best for:** Researchers doing literature reviews. Not general-purpose.

## 7. Wolfram Alpha Pro

**What it is:** Computational knowledge engine with step-by-step solutions.

**Pros:**
- Unmatched for math, science, and engineering
- Step-by-step solutions
- Notebooks for computational analysis
- Import data and images

**Cons:**
- Not a general study assistant
- No document upload or RAG
- No flashcards or quiz generation
- No audio features
- $7.25/mo

**Best for:** STEM students needing computational problem-solving.

AI changes this equation entirely.

## Why Spaced Repetition Works

Spaced repetition works by exploiting the **spacing effect** — your brain retains information better when review sessions are spread out over time, rather than crammed into one session.

The algorithm (FSRS — Free Spaced Repetition Scheduler) calculates the optimal interval between reviews based on:
- **How well you know the card** (ease factor)
- **How many times you've reviewed it** (review count)
- **How long since your last review** (interval)
- **Your historical accuracy** (retention rate)

The result: you review cards **just before you forget them**, maximizing retention with minimum time.

## The Problem with Manual Flashcards

Most students know flashcards work. But they don't use them because:

1. **Time**: Creating 50 flashcards takes 1-2 hours
2. **Quality**: Hand-written cards are often too broad or too specific
3. **Coverage**: You only make cards for topics you think are important
4. **Maintenance**: Updating cards as you learn more is tedious
5. **Format**: Inconsistent card quality makes review less effective

## How AI-Generated Flashcards Solve This

AI can generate flashcards from your documents in seconds. Here's how it works:

### 1. Document Analysis
The AI reads your textbook, lecture notes, or research paper. It identifies:
- Key definitions and concepts
- Cause-and-effect relationships
- Comparisons and contrasts
- Formulas and equations
- Important facts and figures

### 2. Card Generation
For each concept, the AI creates multiple card types:
- **Basic cards**: "What is X?" → "X is..."
- **Cloze cards**: "The mitochondria is the ___ of the cell"
- **Reverse cards**: "ATP" → "Adenosine triphosphate, the energy currency of cells"
- **Comparison cards**: "How do X and Y differ?" → "X does this, while Y does that..."

### 3. Quality Filtering
AI-generated cards are reviewed for:
- Accuracy (does the answer match the source?)
- Clarity (is the question unambiguous?)
- Difficulty (appropriate level for the topic?)
- Coverage (are all important concepts included?)

### 4. Smart Scheduling
Cards are automatically scheduled using FSRS:
- New cards introduced gradually
- Difficult cards shown more frequently
- Easy cards shown less often
- Daily review limits based on your schedule
